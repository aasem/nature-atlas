# Atlas of the Idea of Nature

A hundred and fifty-two texts on nature from Greece and Rome, China, India, Islam, the Bible and Christendom, the Renaissance, the new philosophy of the seventeenth century, its critics, the occult tradition, the perennialists, the twentieth century and the present, each read through one governing question:

> What is nature, where do we stand in it, and what may we do to it?

- `index.html` — the atlas
- `readings.js` — the reader's own reading of each text; edit it directly, or edit on the page and use **Copy readings.js**
